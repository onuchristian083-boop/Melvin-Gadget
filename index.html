iPhone know-how

Your iPhone, clearly explained.

Use this practical guide to understand iOS updates, everyday features, and how to keep your iPhone safe.

Released 2023

iOS 17

A more personal way to communicate and share.

  • StandBy: turn a charging iPhone on its side to show useful information.
  • Contact Posters and NameDrop: personalize your caller card and share contact details nearby.
  • Messages and FaceTime: use features such as Live Voicemail and improved stickers where available.

Released 2024

iOS 18

More ways to personalize and protect your iPhone.

  • Personalization: arrange app icons and widgets, and customize Control Center.
  • Privacy: lock or hide supported apps on compatible devices.
  • Messages: use new text effects and RCS messaging where supported by your carrier and region.
  • Apple Intelligence: available only on supported models, languages, and regions.

Released 2025

iOS 26

Apple moved to year-based version numbers; iOS 19–25 were skipped.

  • Liquid Glass: a refreshed interface across the Lock Screen, Home Screen, and apps.
  • Built-in apps: updates to apps such as Phone and Messages.
  • Apple Intelligence: features depend on a supported iPhone, language, and region.

Released 2026

iOS 27

The newest major iOS generation as of 2026.

  • Features and compatibility depend on your iPhone model, region, language, and the exact 27.x update.
  • Check Apple's current release notes for the full feature list and security fixes as they are published.
  • Point updates: install the newest compatible update for important fixes and security improvements.

Features and update availability depend on your iPhone model, region, language, carrier, and exact iOS version. To see what your phone supports and the newest update available for it, open Settings > General > Software Update. For current details, read Apple’s iOS release notes and update your iPhone.

Good habits

Things you can do

  • Back up your iPhone before a major update or repair.
  • Install iOS updates from Settings, using a trusted Wi-Fi connection and a charger when practical.
  • Use a strong passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and two-factor authentication for your Apple Account.
  • Check battery health, storage, and the phone’s parts and repair history when buying used.
  • Use certified, undamaged charging accessories and keep your iPhone dry unless its specific model rating says otherwise.

Stay safe

Things you should not do

  • Do not share your passcode, Apple Account password, or verification codes with anyone.
  • Do not install configuration profiles, apps, or update files from people or websites you do not trust.
  • Do not interrupt an iOS update or disconnect the phone while it is installing.
  • Do not assume every iPhone supports every iOS feature, or that water resistance means waterproof.
  • Do not erase, trade in, or hand over a phone until you have backed it up and signed out safely.
